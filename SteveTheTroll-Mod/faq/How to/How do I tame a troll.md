How do I tame a troll?

Craft the Fluffy Slippers at the workbench, put them on and stay near a wild troll for about a minute. It becomes yours and is named Steve 1, Steve 2 and so on. You can have one troll near you with the slippers alone, up to 5 with the whole Troll Mascot costume, and never more than 15 living tamed trolls in total.
